What it does — and who it is for

MeetingMint is a meeting transcription app and note generator in one. Press record to capture your microphone, or drag in an audio file you already have — including long recordings of calls, lectures, and interviews. On-device AI transcribes the audio, then writes structured minutes: action items, decisions, and key points.

It goes further than a transcript. You can ask your transcript questions and get answers that cite the exact moment they came from. Everything is organized with search, folders, tags, and bookmarks, and exports to PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML, or plain text.

Built for people whose meetings are not for upload:

  • Teams in legal, HR, healthcare, and research with confidentiality obligations
  • Anyone with confidential recordings that cannot go to a cloud transcription service
  • Journalists and researchers processing interviews
  • People who record long lectures, seminars, or calls and want notes, not just text
  • Anyone who wants AI meeting notes without a subscription-first cloud tool

Why on-device matters for meeting notes

Cloud notetakers hear everything

Most AI meeting tools stream your audio to a server to transcribe it. Meeting content is often the most sensitive data a team produces — personnel matters, client strategy, legal advice, unpublished research. Uploading it to a third party is a real cost, not a technical detail.

Nothing to intercept, nothing to leak

MeetingMint's transcription and note generation run on your Mac. The App Store privacy label says it plainly: Data Not Collected. You can verify the offline behavior yourself — after the one-time model download, it works with the network disconnected.

Your recordings, any length

Because processing is local, long recordings are not a billing problem. Import a three-hour lecture or a month of back-to-back call recordings and batch through them — no per-minute pricing, no upload limits.

Multilingual by default

On-device Whisper supports transcription and minutes in 90+ languages — useful for international teams and interviews without depending on which languages a cloud vendor chooses to support.

Frequently asked questions

Does MeetingMint upload my meetings anywhere?
No. Transcription and note generation run entirely on your Mac using on-device models. There is no cloud service and no account. The App Store privacy label lists Data Not Collected, and the app keeps working offline after the one-time model download.
Can it process Zoom, Teams, or call recordings I already have?
Yes. Import any audio file — a Zoom or Teams recording, a voice memo, an interview, a lecture. This is a deliberate design choice: you bring the audio you already have instead of inviting a bot into live meetings.
Is it an AI meeting note taker that joins meetings for me?
No — it does not join or record your calls on its own. You either record your microphone in the app or import a recording afterwards. Many users prefer exactly this: no bot in the meeting, no third party on the line.
What do the generated notes include?
Clean minutes with action items, decisions, and key points, plus a full transcript. You can also ask the transcript questions and get answers that cite the exact moment they came from.
Which languages are supported?
On-device transcription and minutes work in 90+ languages.
What are the system requirements?
A Mac running macOS 14.0 or later. The app is an 11.5 MB download; AI models are fetched once on first run, after which everything works offline.
How much does it cost?
Free to try with 5 meetings. MeetingMint Pro is $4.99/month or a one-time $79.99 lifetime purchase — and meetings you have already made stay fully usable either way.
Where is my data stored?
On your Mac. Recordings, transcripts, and notes stay in the app's local storage. See the privacy policy for details.
Where do I get it?
